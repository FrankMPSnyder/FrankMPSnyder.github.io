Fitness Events

AFT

The AFT (Army Fitness Test) is a mandatory fitness test all cadets have to take twice a semester. There are five events for the AFT each with a maximum score of 100 points. The events are: the hex bar deadlift, the hand release pushups, the sprint-drag-carry, the plank, and the two mile run. Passing requires a total score of 300 for general, non-combat roles, and a minimum of 350 total points for combat specialties, but if cadets do not score over 440 they must attend remedial PT.

CWST

The CWST (Combat Water Survival Test) is a mandatory once a year test to ensure that cadets will not drown in water. It consists of a fifteen meter swim, a jump off of a three meter platform, and an underwater removal of a slung rifle and FLC tactical vest.

Rucks

A ruck is a forced foot march in uniform with a 35 lb rucksack. Every semester at the mount the required rucks are at least one six mile, and one twelve mile. Passing times are two and four hours respectively.

Training Events

Super Lab

Super Lab is a one day field exercise at Fort Fretterd. Cadets practice warrior skills such as tactical combat casualty care, radios, range cards, shooting, and more. The main event of the day is land navigation, one conducted during the day, and one at night. Cadets are given coordinates to find with a map, protractor, and compass.

FTX/JFTX

FTX (field training exercise) and JFTX joint field training exercise are multiple day training sessions. The difference between the two is FTX is just Green Terror Battalion and JFTX includes other battalions. The first day is generally the same as a super lab. The remaining days are usually tactical operations all day like in labs at school. (see Life During the Schoolweek)

Miscellaneous Events

Commander's Lab

Commander's lab is a battalion meeting once a semester where cadets and cadre talk about the state of the battalion, and opportunities for the Cadets. Any cadet who receives a contract from ROTC, has their contracting ceremony at Commander's Lab. The event ends with a fitness competition between the schools.

Dining In/Out

Dining in is a dinner with all of the battalion cadets at McDaniel at the end of fall semester. This is when the seniors are told what branch they receive in the army when they commission. Dining out is the same, just a different location, and instead of the branching ceremony, new leadership is chosen for the next academic year. These events are also a time for the cadets to have a good time together and build camaraderie.
