Life During the Schoolweek

PT

Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday are PT days. Cadets generally have to show up at Memorial Gym no later than 0550 for PT. PT is led by a different junior every morning and usually targets upper body, lower body, or cardio.

Lab

Lab is every Thursday morning beginning at 0550. This is where cadets practice leadership in tactical operations such as attacks, raids, ambushes, and movement to contact. The juniors take the leadership roles in these operations to prepare for CST camp.

Military Science

Military science is a class taught by the cadre (officers or non-commissioned officers) that are stationed with Green Terror Battalion. Cadets take military science every semester they are in ROTC. The course covers all of the basics of the army that an officer needs to know. Things like Leadership qualities, land navigation, mission planning, and customs and courtesy of the Army.
