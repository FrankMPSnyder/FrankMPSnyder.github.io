Camps & Internships

Internships

Every summer there are opportunities for cadets to apply to Army sponsored internships in a variety of fields. Some of these include cybersecurity, law, and chemistry. Not only do these internships advance a cadet's prospective career in the army, they are a great help for getting civilian jobs as well.

Camps

Generally, The Mount will get a few slots for special camps. Most commonly those camps are Air Assault and OPFOR.

Air Assault

Air Assault School is an intense 10-day course (plus Day 0) that pushes cadets to their physical and mental limits. It begins with Day 0, where cadets must pass a 2-mile run, obstacle course, and gear layout.

The course is divided into three phases:

  • Phase 1 (Days 1–3): Focuses on Air Assault operations, ending with a written exam and a 6-mile ruck march.
  • Phase 2: Teaches sling load inspection; cadets must pass both written and hands-on tests or be dropped.
  • Phase 3: Covers rappelling, including tests on equipment and a rappel from a UH-60 Blackhawk.

The course ends with a 12-mile ruck march and final gear inspection. Those who pass graduate as Air Assault qualified soldiers.

OPFOR

Rising sophomores and juniors Cadets from different ROTC programs across the country stand in as the Opposing Force, or OPFOR, that play the enemy at Advanced Camp Cadets during different FTXs. Not only are they assisting in training, the Cadets also get a chance to catch a glimpse of what's in store for them in the coming years at Advanced Camp.

CST

CST is a 30-35 day course at Ft. Knox, Kentucky. All junior level ROTC cadets go before commissioning. They are tested on their physical, mental and leadership capabilities. The first portion is the garrison portion, which includes PMI and weapons qualification, then land navigation and warrior skills before being tested in the field environment, running STX lanes (like Thursday labs) through each phase of operation.
