Color Guard

The ROTC Color Guard team is a fantastic way to volunteer and proudly present the nation's colors. Practice is held once a week starting with basic rifle and marching movements and works up to semi-complicated maneuvers. Performing at special events, sports games and more, Color guard helps build confidence and professionalism demonstrating in front of a crowd while displaying honor and patriotism to the United States of America.
