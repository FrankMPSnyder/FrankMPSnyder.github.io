Ranger Challenge

Ranger challenge is a team within the battalion only for the most driven cadets. Cadets who join the ranger team are required to do harder PT, PT an extra day of the week, and learn extra skills the ordinary cadet does not get to. All of this is to prepare for the Ranger Challenge Competition where all the battalions in the brigade face off in a competition of fitness, knowledge and skill.
