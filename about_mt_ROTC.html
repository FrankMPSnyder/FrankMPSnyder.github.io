About Mount Saint Mary's ROTC

The Mount Saint Mary’s ROTC program prepares students to become officers in the United States Army. We train excellent cadets through their stay at the Mount by upholding high standards of fitness and leadership.

What cadets do in ROTC

Study military science alongside regular college courses

Attend physical training (PT) four times a week

Join a weekly lab to practice leadership and teamwork

Develop the confidence and discipline needed for Army service
